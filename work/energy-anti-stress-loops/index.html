Energy: Anti-Stress Loops

A relaxing loop-based mobile experience designed for quick, calming sessions.

Overview

Energy: Anti-Stress Loops is a relaxing, loop-based mobile experience built for short, calming sessions.

The technical focus is on responsive touch interaction, clear feedback, and predictable performance on a wide range of mobile devices.

I worked as a Unity developer, implementing the interaction loop and keeping the runtime lightweight and stable for production shipping.

What I Worked On

  • Implemented the core loop interaction mechanics (touch input, selection/connection rules, and validation).
  • Built feedback and animation hooks to keep interactions readable and satisfying without overloading the UI.
  • Managed session flow and state transitions to support quick play sessions and interruption-safe behavior.
  • Profiled and optimized for mobile performance, focusing on frame pacing and low CPU overhead.
  • Ensured behavior stayed consistent across common screen sizes and aspect ratios.
  • Supported production maintenance work: bug triage, edge-case fixes, and stability improvements.

Technical Notes

  • Kept interaction evaluation lightweight and deterministic to avoid “almost works” edge cases that frustrate players.
  • Handled input smoothing and thresholding so touch feels stable without introducing lag.
  • Minimized allocations and per-frame work in hot paths to keep the experience calm (no stutters).
  • Isolated rules/state from presentation so UI changes didn’t risk breaking gameplay validation.

Outcome / Impact

  • Shipped to production on Google Play.
  • Delivered responsive interactions with stable performance across typical mobile devices.
  • Supported ongoing maintenance updates focused on stability and polish.
