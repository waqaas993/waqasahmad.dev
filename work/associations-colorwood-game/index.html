Associations - Colorwood Game

A shipped Unity word association puzzle focused on category discovery, calm puzzle pacing, and scalable mobile systems across iOS and Android.

Google Play App Store

Overview

Associations - Colorwood Game is a mobile word puzzle where each level asks players to uncover thematic connections between words. My work fits the product-systems side of the project: gameplay behavior, hint flows, live updates, monetization surfaces, analytics, and production stability.

Tech stack

Built in the Unity mobile stack, with the usual production concerns around iOS/Android builds, remote configuration, analytics, ad monetization, IAP, localization, and post-launch iteration.

What I focused on

I worked on systems that support a live mobile puzzle product rather than one-off screens: progression, hint/support flows, event-driven UI behavior, monetization integrations, debugging instrumentation, and reliability fixes for edge cases seen in production.

Why it matters

The project shows how I approach mobile products at scale: keep the player loop simple, make systems configurable, instrument the risky paths, and build recovery paths for the weird production cases that only appear after real users get involved.
