Idle Dot Shooter

A minimalist idle shooter across 50 galaxies, with bosses, special dots, and upgradeable weapons. I joined the live team to harden monetization, platform compliance, and player data.

Google Play App Store

Overview

Players deploy auto-firing shooters that destroy dots for money, upgrade weapons and powers, and travel between galaxies. The game runs on a Canvas 2D loop inside a React shell, with offline earnings, daily missions, streaks, a galaxy map, leaderboards, and contextual offers.

What I focused on

Cloud save with transfer codes, linked-device sync, and an identity migration; iOS ATT and notification prompt ordering and GDPR/TCF consent reporting; ad and attribution SDK fixes, including double-counted IAP revenue; reinstall-stable A/B segments via Remote Config; Android stability fixes for crashes and thread exhaustion; a rewarded offer-wall integration with placement variants and e2e tests; and several store releases.

Tech stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Capacitor 8, Supabase, Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), AppLovin MAX, AppsFlyer, devtodev, i18next across 10 locales, Playwright and Vitest, and Fastlane.

Why it matters

Live games are won or lost on the unglamorous layer: consent, attribution accuracy, crash rates, and never losing a player’s progress. This work is about keeping a shipped product trustworthy while it keeps changing.
