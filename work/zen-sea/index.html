Zen Sea: Idle Fish Game

A portrait arcade roguelite where you play a shark hunting fish schools whose behavior evolves each generation. I joined for a focused pass on monetization, analytics, and player flow.

In development

Overview

The predator hunts on its own while taps trigger stamina-limited dashes. Each fish is steered by a small neural network; survivors of a wave breed a smarter next generation. Pearls buy permanent upgrades between runs.

What I focused on

Placing interstitials only at run end and keeping banners off gameplay, joining offer impressions to their outcomes in analytics, tracking evolve prompts and repeat runs, asking for push permission at the end of the tutorial with recovery from Settings, and making “New Run” the obvious next action after game over.

Tech stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Canvas 2D, Zustand, Framer Motion, Capacitor 8, Firebase, and a shared in-house SDK kit for ads, attribution, and analytics.
